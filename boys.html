BOYS HOSTELS

Welcome to the Boys Hostels of Guru Nanak Dev Engineering College. The hostels provide students with a safe, comfortable, and disciplined living environment along with modern facilities for academic and personal growth.

Hostel Information

🏠 Accommodation

Spacious single, double, and triple-sharing rooms with beds, study tables, fans, and cupboards.

📶 Wi-Fi Facility

High-speed internet connectivity is available throughout the hostel campus.

🔐 Security

24×7 security staff, CCTV surveillance, and strict hostel discipline ensure student safety.

🍽 Mess Facility

Hygienic and nutritious breakfast, lunch, and dinner are provided daily.

📚 Study Rooms

Quiet study areas and reading rooms help students maintain academic focus.

⚡ Power Backup

Continuous electricity supply with power backup support inside hostels.
