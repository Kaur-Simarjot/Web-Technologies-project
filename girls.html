GIRLS HOSTELS

The Girls Hostels at Guru Nanak Dev Engineering College provide a safe, secure, and peaceful environment for students. The hostel promotes academic excellence, discipline, and comfortable living with modern facilities and caring supervision.

Hostel Information

🏠 Comfortable Rooms

Spacious and clean rooms with proper ventilation, study tables, beds, cupboards, and fans.

🛡 Safety & Security

24×7 security staff, CCTV monitoring, biometric entry, and hostel wardens ensure student safety.

📶 Internet Access

High-speed Wi-Fi connectivity is available for academic learning and communication.

🍽 Hygienic Mess

Healthy and nutritious meals are prepared daily with proper hygiene and quality standards.

📚 Study Environment

Dedicated study rooms and reading areas help students maintain concentration and discipline.

⚡ Power Backup

Continuous electricity supply and backup facilities are available throughout the hostel.
